# Everyday consumer/citizen pain points in Tunisia, 2024–2026 (catalogue of paid-workaround problems)

> **Method and reliability note (read first).** Research date 2026-11-14. All findings below come from web-search result summaries and snippets. Direct page fetches of Tunisian press sites (businessnews.com.tn, lapresse.tn, managers.tn, fr.allafrica.com) were blocked by this session's network policy, so **no article was read in full** and figures were not re-checked against full text. Where a search summary did not say which of several listed URLs a figure came from, the citation is marked "(URL attribution approximate)". No Reddit r/Tunisia threads, Facebook-group posts or Google Play reviews could be retrieved; no user quote from those sources is reproduced. Verbatim phrases in quotation marks are snippet text returned by search, not full-context quotes. Labels used: **[Verified-press]** = reported figure attributed to a named institution/person in press; **[Stakeholder claim]** = figure from an interested party (syndicate, trade chamber, vendor); **[Anecdotal]**; **[Inference]**.

## 1. Public administration: documents, digitisation, queues, intermediaries

### Takeaway
Digitisation is real but partial and repeatedly delayed: Mobile ID/e-Houwiya and online birth extracts exist, but biometric CIN/passport, remote passport/CIN applications and the "Khadamet" super-app (40+ services) were still announced-not-delivered as of Aug 2026. The best-documented paid intermediary market is not for Tunisian documents but for foreign visa appointments, where slots are resold for hundreds of dinars.

### Cited Findings
- **Digitised:** a birth-certificate extract can be requested online through e-Houwiya with an active Mobile ID account; Mobile-ID gives access to e-services of public and private institutions. [Verified – procedure portal] — [Idaraty e-Houwiya extrait](https://idaraty.tn/fr/procedures/e-houwiya-extrait-naissance); [Idaraty Mobile-ID](https://idaraty.tn/public/fr/procedures/e-houwiya-identite-digitale-mobile-id-tunisie)
- An (undated, probably older) Kapitalis piece criticised the online birth extract: the full/integral extract was not available in the system and online fees exceeded counter fees. May be outdated. — [Kapitalis](https://kapitalis.com/tunisie/?p=3169293)
- **Still pending:** biometric passport/CIN were promised for H1 2025, then H2 2025, then "from 2026" starting with diplomats and frequent travellers; a La Presse piece of Oct 2025 asks when the ordinary citizen will get one. — [Webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-la-cin-et-le-passeport-biometriques-des-le-premier-semestre-de-2025/213726/); [Le Courrier de l'Atlas](https://www.lecourrierdelatlas.com/?p=200277); [La Presse 15/10/2025](https://lapresse.tn/2025/10/15/a-quand-le-passeport-biometrique-pour-le-citoyen-lambda/)
- Remote application/renewal of CIN and passport was targeted for 31 Dec 2026 (Business News); fees announced "from 100 dinars" for the two biometric documents. (URL attribution approximate) — [Business News](https://businessnews.com.tn/?p=1427550); [Tunisie Numérique](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/carte-didentite-biometrique-ce-qui-va-changer-pour-les-tunisiens/)
- Government labelled 2026 the year of "complete digitisation", tracking 192 digital projects; in-progress items include remote residence certificate, online driving-licence renewal, generalised remote passport. — [Webmanagercenter 14/02/2026](https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/02/14/561763/tunisie-2026-annee-du-lancement-de-la-digitalisation-complete-de-ladministration/)
- The "Khadamet" mobile app (40+ administrative services) was announced as imminent but not yet launched on 15 Aug 2026. — [La Presse 15/08/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/15/plus-de-40-services-administratifs-sur-une-seule-application-ce-qui-attend-les-tunisiens/)
- Le Courrier de l'Atlas notes roll-out is held back by interconnection, inter-administration coordination and service-quality problems. — [Le Courrier de l'Atlas](https://www.lecourrierdelatlas.com/?p=215808)
- **Paid intermediaries (visa appointments):** consumer body OTIC says Schengen appointments obtained on official platforms are resold for "several hundred dinars" on social networks/informal intermediaries; other reporting says up to thousands. A 2023 Business News report found an intermediary selling a "VIP" appointment for 850 TND agency fee on top of official costs. In May–June 2026 the French consul general and OTIC agreed to cooperate against resale networks. Official costs: €90 (~305 TND) visa fee plus TLScontact service fee (reported as ~95 TND in one source, €28 in another — conflicting). — [La Presse 16/05/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/05/16/visas-lotic-denonce-les-derives-des-societes-intermediaires-et-appelle-a-la-reciprocite/); [Managers 02/06/2026](https://managers.tn/2026/06/02/le-consul-general-et-lotoc-unissent-leurs-efforts-contre-la-revente-illegale-de-rendez-vous-visa/); [Business News 2023](https://businessnews.com.tn/2023/04/29/article-1197311/1197311/); [AllAfrica/La Presse 17/09/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609170528.html); [Lesafriques](https://lesafriques.com/2026/09/visa-france-tunisie-demarches-delais-couts-2026/)
- **Bribes for documents (old data):** Afrobarometer 2018: 12% of those who sought identity documents paid a bribe at least once. No 2024–25 equivalent found. — [Afrobarometer 2018](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/tun_r7_pr1_corruption_01102018.pdf)
- Syndic committees face an "administrative absurdity" over patente and RNE (company register) obligations that discourages volunteer co-owners (opinion tribune, Aug 2026). — [La Presse tribune](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/16/tribune-patente-syndic-et-rne-labsurdite-administrative/)
- Idaraty.tn exists as a private/civic procedures-information portal, itself evidence of demand for clear procedural guidance. — [Idaraty](https://idaraty.tn/fr)

### Inferences
- [Inference] The willingness to pay 300–850+ TND merely for an appointment slot shows Tunisians pay meaningful sums to bypass queue/booking friction; a legal "dossier preparation + monitoring" service is plausible, but slot resale itself is being actively targeted by consulates.
- [Inference] Because government keeps announcing but slipping (biometric ID, Khadamet), any business built on a specific paper bottleneck carries regulatory-obsolescence risk; durable value is in assistance/representation (diaspora, elderly, SMEs), not in the bottleneck itself.

### Gaps
- No source found on online B3 (bulletin n°3), carte grise/ATTT appointments, legalisation of signature, land registry (CPF) delays, CNSS procedures, or customs clearance of personal parcels (the search returned only French/Belgian material).
- No evidence retrieved (for or against) of paid "runner"/coursier administratif services for Tunisian documents, nor their prices. Likely exists informally but unverified.
- No Google Play/App Store review text for E-Houwiya or other public apps could be retrieved.
- TUNEPS and e-bawaba status not covered by any result.

## 2. Healthcare: shortages, CNAM, doctor exodus, elder care, mental health

### Takeaway
Three compounding, well-documented failures create paid workarounds: recurring medicine shortages (patients source via Facebook groups, travellers and a parallel market), a CNAM payment crisis that suspended third-party payment in private pharmacies from late 2025 (patients pay cash upfront and file for reimbursement), and a physician exodus of roughly 1,000–1,500 a year. Elder care is the clearest structural gap: 2M+ people aged 60+ (16.9%) with an acknowledged lack of home-care aides.

### Cited Findings
**Medicine shortages**
- Press describes a decade-long problem ("un calvaire de 10 ans"); La Presse/AllAfrica headline (Sept 2025): 90% of drugs in shortage have no substitute in the country. — [AllAfrica 08/08/2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202508080524.html); [AllAfrica 02/09/2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202509020231.html)
- Most affected: thyroid, some cancer and psychiatric treatments; chronic patients hit hardest. Pharmacists attribute it to the Pharmacie centrale's financial difficulties because CNAM has not paid its debts; subsidised drugs are also smuggled to Algeria/Libya. — [L'Economiste Maghrébin 01/09/2025](https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/09/01/le-cnopt-alerte-sur-la-penurie-de-medicaments-importes/); [Webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-penurie-de-medicaments-importes-une-fragilite-qui-touche-les-malades-chroniques/382523/) (URL attribution approximate)
- **Workaround:** pharmacists' syndicate representative Molka Al Moudir links trafficking to Tunisians buying medicines via social networks because of "severe" shortage in official pharmacies, warning of uncontrolled products. [Stakeholder claim] — [Business News 26/08/2025](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/08/26/penurie-mortelle-de-medicaments-les-solutions-sont-la-mais-letat-les-enterre/1361469/) (URL attribution approximate)
- **Workaround:** Facebook group "Médicaments Pour Tous" was created by a patient after a distress call led Tunisians to bring her medicine from abroad; another page "Winou_eddewe" ("where is the medicine?") had around 7,000 members (article undated in results; figures may be old). Kapitalis: Facebook groups and community initiatives to find medicines have become more common. [Anecdotal/press] — [The New Arab](https://www.newarab.com/analysis/tunisians-struggle-life-saving-medicine-shortage-hits-country); [Kapitalis](https://kapitalis.com/tunisie/?p=8939464)
- 2026 persistence: testimonies on missing cancer drugs (Feb 2026), a vital transplant drug unavailable (Feb 2026), pharmacists again warning of stock-outs incl. immunosuppressants (Sept 2026); ministry injected 15M TND and promised a list of vital medicines (Sept 2026). — [Business News 04/02/2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/02/04/penuries-de-medicaments-essentiels-au-traitement-du-cancer-des-temoignages-qui-denoncent-une-crise-sanitaire-et-ethique/1386648/); [Business News 02/02/2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/02/02/patients-greffes-en-detresse-un-medicament-vital-introuvable/1386281/); [AllAfrica 30/09/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609300563.html); [AllAfrica 16/09/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609160567.html)
- Ministry plan includes an early-warning platform at the Pharmacie centrale (supply-side only; no patient-facing drug locator mentioned). — [L'Economiste Maghrébin 24/08/2025](https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/08/24/tunisie-strategie-nationale-penurie-medicaments/)

**CNAM**
- Pharmacists' syndicate (SPOT) suspended third-party payment for ordinary illnesses from 1 Oct 2025, then more broadly from 8 Dec 2025 after talks failed; >90% of pharmacies complained of CNAM late payment; ~30% of affiliates affected. — [Webdo 16/09/2025](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/?p=383652); [La Presse 28/10/2025](https://lapresse.tn/2025/10/28/plus-de-90-des-pharmacies-se-plaignent-du-retard-de-paiement-de-la-cnam/); [Business News 09/12/2025](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/12/09/tiers-payant-suspendu-les-pharmacies-tunisiennes-au-bord-de-lasphyxie-financiere/1377552/)
- Workaround imposed on patients: CNAM set up "exceptional reimbursement" — insured pays cash and files a dossier with prescription, invoice and medicine stickers. — [Business News 10/12/2025](https://businessnews.com.tn/?p=1377825); [Tunisie Numérique](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/cnam-remboursement-exceptionnel-des-medicaments-apres-la-suspension-du-tiers-payant-dans-les-pharmacies-privees/)

- Still unresolved Nov 2026: La Presse headlines "CNAM: cotiser toujours, attendre indéfiniment" (5 Nov 2026) and "Pharmacies privées: les impayés de la CNAM pèsent de plus en plus lourd" (2 Nov 2026). — [La Presse 05/11/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/05/cnam-cotiser-toujours-attendre-indefiniment/); [La Presse 02/11/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/02/pharmacies-privees-les-impayes-de-la-cnam-pesent-de-plus-en-plus-lourd/)

**Access, bribes, exodus**
- Afrobarometer (AD976, 2025): 47% used a public health facility in past 12 months; 63% of users found it difficult/very difficult to get care; ~18% paid a bribe/gift; 81% worry about not obtaining/affording care; only 37% approve of government performance on health. — [AllAfrica/Afrobarometer AD976](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202505050294.html)

**Elder care**
- 60+ population: 16.88% (>2 million) vs 11.38% in 2014; press says ageing exposes a lack of auxiliaires de vie and specialised home services. — [La Presse 03/07/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/03/tunisie-un-pays-qui-vieillit-plus-vite-que-prevu/); [Managers 29/09/2025](https://managers.tn/2025/09/29/la-tunisie-compte-plus-de-2-millions-de-personnes-agees/)

- Public home-protection programme: 49 mobile teams in 19 governorates covering only ~5,000 people (2025). — [Webmanagercenter 01/10/2025](https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/10/01/552712/soutien-aux-seniors-en-tunisie-programmes-de-protection-placement-familial-et-equipes-mobiles/)
- Home aide wages: 500–800 TND/month net beginner, 1,200–1,800 TND senior (job-board blog, unofficial). Private medicalised residences targeting European retirees from €1,200/month. — [Tanitjobs blog](https://www.tanitjobs.com/blog/1126/emploi-aide-domicile-auxiliaire-vie-agent-entretien-proprete-services-personne-tunisie-2026); [La Presse 15/08/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/15/retraites-europeens-la-tunisie-developpe-une-offre-de-residences-medicalisees-a-des-prix-competitifs/)

**Mental health**
- ~200,000 psychiatric consultations a year (Ordre president Dr Rim Ghachem, Apr 2026); Razi hospital consultations +6% and emergencies +9% in 2025; tele-psychiatry made a ministry priority after a first Sfax–Tataouine remote consultation (Sept 2025); access to psychiatric care "remains a major challenge" (Oct 2026). — [La Presse 27/04/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/04/27/avec-200-000-rendez-vous-medicaux-par-an-consulter-un-psychiatre-nest-plus-tabou-en-tunisie/); [Webmanagercenter 12/10/2026](https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/10/12/574527/sante-mentale-en-tunisie-pourquoi-lacces-aux-soins-psychiatriques-reste-un-defi-majeur/)

### Inferences
- [Inference] Medicine search is a high-frequency, high-anxiety problem with a proven informal workaround (Facebook groups, travellers' suitcases) but no trustworthy legal tool; a pharmacy-stock locator or regulated import-on-prescription concierge would address it, though drug sales outside pharmacies are illegal and regulation is the binding constraint.
- [Inference] Since Dec 2025 many insured patients must advance cash and assemble paper CNAM dossiers; claims-filing assistance and complementary insurance/health financing have a newly enlarged addressable base (~30% of affiliates per Business News).
- [Inference] Elder home care combines a demographic certainty, visible supply gap and a payer with foreign currency (diaspora children). Low caregiver wages (500–1,800 TND) suggest room for a managed-agency margin; no public price list exists.

### Gaps
- No data on what patients actually pay on the parallel medicine market, nor current size/activity of the Facebook groups.
- No tariffs found for private home nursing, auxiliaire de vie agencies, psychologists or psychiatrists; no count of psychiatrists.
- Nothing retrieved on lab-result delivery or doctor appointment-booking platforms (e.g., adoption of existing booking apps), or on specialist availability by region.
- No evidence retrieved specifically documenting diaspora paying for parents' care (plausible, unverified).

## 3. Education: tutoring, exam prep, language certification, childcare

### Takeaway
Private tutoring is the largest documented household "workaround spend": estimates of 1.5–3 billion TND/year nationally and up to 15,000 TND for one bac candidate, with a ban bill in preparation (regulatory risk). Preschool care is the second: only ~47% of 3–5-year-olds are in licensed kindergartens, with fees cited above 470 TND/month and a price-cap bill under discussion.

### Cited Findings
- Tutoring market: Business News (May 2026) puts it at 1.5–3 billion TND (up to 20% of the Education Ministry budget); La Presse (Aug 2026) cites ~3 billion TND (~40% of education budget) — the budget share is disputed; an FTDES study (Sept 2023) estimated 1.468 billion TND/year. [Stakeholder/press estimates; FTDES most rigorous] — [Business News 30/05/2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/05/30/une-proposition-de-loi-en-preparation-pour-bannir-les-cours-particuliers/1403922/); [La Presse 29/08/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/29/rentree-scolaire-jusqua-800-dinars-de-depenses-et-une-pression-croissante-sur-les-familles/) (URL attribution approximate for FTDES figure)
- Household level: parents' association president Ridha Zahrouni says some families pay up to 15,000 TND in one school year for a bac pupil; monthly spend 300–600 TND per pupil, with claims up to 2,000 TND; La Presse 2025 headline "up to 2,000 dinars to pass the bac"; a Tunisie Numérique family survey gives ~100 TND/child/month on average. Some households reportedly cut food/basic spending to pay. — [Business News 06/05/2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/05/06/temoignage-jusqua-15-000-dinars-de-cours-particuliers-pour-un-eleve-du-baccalaureat/1400141/); [La Presse 10/05/2025](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/05/10/jusqua-2-000-dinars-pour-reussir-le-bac-les-cours-particuliers-hors-de-controle-en-tunisie/); [Tunisie Numérique](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/cout-de-la-rentree-scolaire-entre-maitrise-des-prix-et-exigences-familiales/)

- Back-to-school cost up to 800 TND per pupil (2026). — [Webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/rentree-scolaire-jusqua-800-dinars-par-eleve-la-facture-salourdit-pour-les-tunisiens/403300/)
- Childcare: only 47% of preschool-age children are in legal kindergartens (53% outside the legal network); 133 closure decisions against unlicensed structures in Jan–Oct 2025; ~7,500 children in crèches nationally and several governorates have no crèche; private sector = 94% of kindergartens; 500 establishments reported closed. — [Business News 15/09/2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/09/15/jardins-denfants-53-des-enfants-de-3-a-5-ans-hors-du-reseau-legal/1418527/); [Business News 08/01/2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/01/08/petite-enfance-133-creches-et-garderies-non-conformes-fermes-entre-janvier-et-octobre-2025/1381887/); [AllAfrica 24/04/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202604240648.html)

[Stakeholder claim] — [Business News 01/09/2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/09/01/nabila-miladi-le-cout-dun-enfant-en-jardin-denfants-depasse-470-dinars-par-mois/1416413/); [AllAfrica 09/10/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610090360.html)

- Language tests: IELTS paper test listed at 620 TND at British Council Tunis (third-party site, undated). — [BestMyTest](https://www.bestmytest.com/ko/ielts/test-centre/tunisia/tunis)
- Business News ran "the real cost of studying abroad for Tunisians" (July 2026) — content not retrieved. — [Business News 17/07/2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/07/17/le-vrai-cout-des-etudes-a-letranger-pour-les-tunisiens/1410485/)

### Inferences
- [Inference] Tutoring demand is price-inelastic (families cut essentials) and concentrated on bac years; online/group formats at 100–300 TND/month undercut the 300–600 TND in-person norm, but a ban law, if passed, could either kill informal supply (benefiting licensed platforms) or restrict the whole category.
- [Inference] 53% of 3–5-year-olds outside licensed structures plus near-absence of crèches (0–3) indicates unmet demand for affordable licensed childcare; a tariff cap would compress margins.

## 4. Employment, emigration, freelancing and payments

### Takeaway
Graduate unemployment is stuck at 22–25% (32% for women graduates), fuelling a paid emigration-preparation market in which agencies reportedly charge up to 30,000 TND for services available far cheaper. Freelancers still cannot receive PayPal payments (Tunisia is "send only") and the auto-entrepreneur regime has enrolled only ~9–10k people in 18 months.

### Cited Findings
- INS: unemployment 15.0% in Q1 2026; higher-education graduates 24.2% (up from 22.5% in Q4 2025); women graduates 32% vs men 14.2%. Graduate rate was 24.9% in Q3 2025. — [Managers 15/05/2026](https://managers.tn/2026/05/15/chomage-en-tunisie-le-taux-baisse-a-15-mais-le-chomage-des-diplomes-grimpe-a-242/); [Tunisie Numérique Q4 2025](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/tunisie-le-taux-de-chomage-recule-a-152-au-quatrieme-trimestre-2025/); [La Presse 15/11/2025](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/11/15/tunisie-le-chomage-grimpe-a-154-au-troisieme-trimestre-2025/)
- Emigration agencies: association TuniBless's president warned of rising scams; some agencies demand up to 30,000 TND for services obtainable at far lower cost (Managers headline, Apr 2026: "30 000 dinars pour aller en Allemagne"). A headline claims 90+ foreign-recruitment agencies operate illegally (not verified in article body). — [Managers 20/04/2026](https://managers.tn/2026/04/20/30-000-dinars-pour-aller-en-allemagne-pourquoi-autant-de-jeunes-tunisiens-veulent-ils-partir/); [Tunisie Numérique](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/1090377-2/)

## 5. Housing and daily life

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced paid problems here are (a) loss of instalment credit after the Feb 2025 cheque law (29% of consumers postponed or dropped a purchase), (b) rising rents with a rent-cap bill pending, (c) chronic water cuts and now electricity load-shedding driving storage/solar/battery purchases, and (d) an ageing car fleet (half over 15 years) with costly spare parts. Evidence on handymen, syndic and listings quality is thin.

### Cited Findings
**Consumer credit after the cheque law**
- Law n°41-2024 took effect 2 Feb 2025, ending post-dated cheques widely used for instalment purchases; merchants stopped multi-cheque "facilités"; transition was chaotic (old cheque forms refused before new ones issued). — [Business News 28/01/2025](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/01/28/les-anciens-cheques-ne-sont-plus-acceptes-les-nouveaux-ne-sont-pas-encore-delivres-cest-le-chaos/1352513/); [Business News 15/01/2025](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/01/15/banques-commercants-citoyens-tous-victimes-de-la-nouvelle-loi-sur-les-cheques/1352039/)
- IACE consumer note one month after: 29% of consumers gave up or postponed a planned purchase; among middle-income households (1,000–3,000 TND) 88% gave up a purchase (as reported in search summary — check base/denominator in the PDF); a third of consumers saw the cheque purely as deferred payment. — [IACE note (Leaders PDF)](https://leaders.com.tn/uploads/FCK_files/Note-dAnalyse-_-Evaluation-de-la-nouvelle-reglementation-des-cheques-aupres-des-consommateurs.pdf)
- Furniture manufacturer: most sales were on credit to young couples with no alternative; cash became the preferred means of payment (May 2025). ## 6. Diaspora needs

### Takeaway
The diaspora is a large, hard-currency payer (≈9 billion TND remitted in 2025, >7% of GDP) and official bodies explicitly acknowledge fragmented information and under-digitised services, but direct evidence on demand for property management, parents' care or construction supervision was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Remittances: record 8,506M TND in 2024 (BCT via Webdo); ≈9 billion TND in 2025, a little over 7% of GDP (OTE); 3.16 billion TND reported by mid-July 2026. — [La Presse 11/05/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/05/11/tunisiens-residant-a-letranger-pres-de-9-milliards-de-dinars-transferes-en-2025/); [Webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/?p=380517); [AllAfrica 15/07/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202607150184.html)
- Diaspora and tourism together cover ~95% of debt service (APA headline). — [APA](https://fr.apanews.net/news/tunisie-95-du-service-de-la-dette-couverts-par-la-diaspora-et-le-tourisme/)
- OTE is digitising services and launched the "Tunisiens du Monde" administrative platform; July 2026 recommendations called for a unified information space across institutions and more digitised diaspora services. — [La Presse 11/05/2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/05/11/tunisiens-residant-a-letranger-pres-de-9-milliards-de-dinars-transferes-en-2025/); [AllAfrica 15/07/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202607150184.html)
- BNA–OTE partnership targets diaspora investment incl. real estate; a platform for foreign-currency investment by non-residents was announced (July 2025). — [AllAfrica 30/07/2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202507300257.html); [AllAfrica 23/07/2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202507230164.html)

- [Anecdotal] An expat forum on building in Djerba/Hammamet advises being constantly on site or delegating to a site manager. — [Expat.com](https://www.expat.com/fr/forum/afrique/tunisie/hammamet/1041725-construction-maison-hammamet.html)

### Inferences
- [Inference] The 50% purchase drop caused by misinformation shows diaspora decisions are highly sensitive to trust/information — a paid, accountable "one-stop" concierge (paperwork by proxy, property management, construction reporting, parents' care coordination) fits, analogous to services marketed to Moroccans abroad (search returned Moroccan MRE construction-supervision guides, none Tunisian — suggesting the Tunisian niche is less served, or simply less visible online).
- [Inference] Combining sections 2 and 6: elder home care paid in EUR by children abroad is the most defensible diaspora product.

### Gaps
- No Tunisian source quantifying diaspora demand for property management, construction supervision, procuration services or parents' care; no pricing of existing providers; remittance fees not retrieved; diaspora population figure not sourced in this research.
