# Tunisian startup and digital-services ecosystem: competitor map by sector (2024–2026)

> Method note (read first): research date 2026-11-14. In this session, direct page fetches failed for every host tried (launchbaseafrica.com, noqta.tn, businessnews.com.tn, fr.allafrica.com were outside the session's network allowlist). All findings below therefore come from web-search result extracts, not from full-page reads. Gap classes used: (1) no known local competitor, (2) competitors exist but market underserved, (3) solutions exist with a significant weakness, (4) successful foreign model not properly localised.

## 1. Startup Act / Startup Tunisia: labels, funding 2023–2026, exits and closures, Startup Act 2.0, funds

### Takeaway
The label count kept growing (1,146 by Feb 2025; later sources say 1,165, 1,352 and "over 1,450", not reconciled), but venture funding collapsed: about $39–42M in 2025, then near zero disclosed equity in H1 2026. Startup Act 2.0 was still a draft/under examination in 2026; the two big exits (InstaDeep, Expensya, both 2023) were realised through non-Tunisian holding entities.

### Inferences
- A founder in 2026 should assume almost no local VC money beyond 216 Capital six-figure tickets, grants (GreenTech, donor programmes) and revenue; capital-light, cash-generating models fit the environment better than blitzscaling.
- The label count (cumulative grants) overstates the live population: aggregators record hundreds of shut-downs, and labels expire after 8 years.
- Local competitors in most sectors are small and thinly funded, so "competitors exist" rarely means "well-resourced incumbent".

## 2. Existing players by sector (what exists, model, traction, weaknesses, status) and gap classification

### Takeaway
Crowded: job boards, electronics e-commerce, payment gateways, bank-led BNPL, restaurant delivery (Glovo-dominated). Vacated or legally frozen: general-merchandise marketplace (Jumia gone), ride-hailing (Bolt gone, no legal framework), online grocery (Founa closed). Regulation-driven openings in 2026: mandatory e-invoicing (El Fatoora/TTN) and mandatory connected cash registers for all cafés/restaurants since 1 July 2026. Thin or near-empty: auto services, property management, insurance comparison, SME payroll/HR, home services at quality, solar-installer and recycling marketplaces.

## 3. App Store / Google Play ratings and recurring complaints for main Tunisian apps

### Takeaway
The few listings retrieved show poor ratings: government identity app E-Houwiya about 1.6 stars, big bank apps between 2.4 and 3.4. Complaints centre on login/authentication failures, crashes, "service unavailable" errors, slow connections, fees and unresponsive support.

### Cited Findings
- E-Houwiya (mobile digital identity): about 1.6 stars on 707 reviews; complaints of empty citizen-portal page, no authentication notification received, rejection after verification interview — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tn.com.progress.ehouwiya&hl=en_US); on Google Play since March 2024 — [Kapitalis](https://kapitalis.com/tunisie/2024/03/21/lapplication-tunisienne-e-houwiya-disponible-sur-google-playstore/)
- Khadamet, the all-in-one government services app, depends on E-Houwiya, whose adoption "remains a work in progress" — [Carthage Magazine](https://carthagemagazine.com/khadamet-app-tunisia/)
- Attijari Mobile Tunisie: 2.4 stars, 3.77K reviews; transfers failing due to bugs, contested monthly fees (TND 15 cited), crashes, slow customer service — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tn.com.attijarirealtime.mobile&hl=en_US)
- MyBIAT: 2.8 stars, about 8.7K ratings, 500K+ downloads; crashes at access-code confirmation — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tn.com.biat.mybiat&hl=en_US)
- Attijari UP: 2.9 stars, 663 reviews; "Service indisponible" after PIN entry — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tn.attijari.android.prod&hl=en_US)
- BT Mobile (Banque de Tunisie): 3.4 stars, 283 reviews; slow login, PIN re-entry each time, update degraded UX — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tn.banquedetunisie.btmobileapp&hl=en_US)
- D17 app listing exists on the App Store (rating not captured) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/digipostbank-d17/id1475640303)

### Inferences
- Reliability and onboarding, not features, are the weak point of incumbent bank and government apps; a product that simply works at login and payment has a visible quality advantage.
- Services that sit on top of E-Houwiya inherit its onboarding friction.

### Gaps
- No ratings or reviews retrieved for TUNEPS, e-CNSS, Tej, MyOoredoo, My Orange, Tunisie Telecom, Glovo, Yassir, Flouci or Konnect apps.
- Ratings are point-in-time from search extracts; review dates were inconsistent across listing pages. App Store (iOS) ratings not captured.

## 4. Which sectors show almost no credible local product

### Takeaway
No credible local product was found for: consumer insurance comparison/digital brokerage, used-car transactions with inspection and financing, garage booking and spare-parts e-commerce, packaged syndic/rental-management SaaS, and merchant-neutral BNPL. Legal ride-hailing is empty by enforcement, not by lack of demand.

### Cited Findings
- Auto: only a classifieds portal and one dealer-brand app surfaced — [Automobile.tn](https://www.automobile.tn/fr/magazine/actu/2026-07-29-nimr-automobile-lance-karhabty-pour-dongfeng-et-dfsk-en-tunisie.html); regional models exist abroad — [Managers.tn](https://managers.tn/2025/05/21/une-plateforme-egyptienne-pour-le-marche-des-voitures-doccasion-leve-157-millions-de-dollars/)

- Property management: only custom-development offers found — [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-gestion-syndic-copropriete-tunis-prix-2026)
- Ride-hailing: main platforms removed in 2025, regulation still pending April 2026 — [Business News](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/04/20/taxis-le-ministre-promet-une-regulation-un-an-apres-le-seisme-des-vtc/1397477/)

### Inferences
- Best-evidenced openings (demand forced by regulation, low capital): compliant POS for cafés/restaurants and e-invoicing connectors for e-commerce and accountants; both have a 2026 legal trigger, though e-invoicing is crowding quickly.
- Empty-because-hard: ride-hailing (legal risk), B2B retail distribution (Kamioun's difficulty), online grocery (Founa's economics), licensed fintech (TND 5M capital).
- Empty-and-plausible: auto services, syndic/rental SaaS, insurance comparison, SME payroll; these need direct market checks because the evidence is absence of search hits.

### Gaps
- Could not verify emptiness through app-store category browsing or company registries; conclusions rest on press and search coverage only.
- Certification requirements and list of approved cash-register software vendors (DGI) not found.
